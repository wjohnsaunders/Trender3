# Door Hinges

These door hinges are designed to fit to a 2040 (or 4040) V-Slot extrusion and
allow for the door to swing open 270 degrees and clear panels and panel clips
(7mm clearance provided - which is a Trident 4mm panel clip).

Rather than use adhesive tape, they are designed for holes to be drilled in the
panels, with the hinge screwed on using a reinforcement part to sandwitch the
panel and prevent it cracking.

# Printing

Print 2 or 3 sets for the left, 2 or 3 sets for the right, and the appropriate
number of reinforcement parts.

There are a couple of drill jigs that you can print to guide hole drilling. The
end jig provides 16mm from the edge of the panel (6mm is the overlap of the
extrusion, and 10mm for spacing). However you can use the other jig to place
the holes wherever you want them. Just measure and check clearance to any
other parts that you might have before drilling. If your panel clips are
thicker than 7mm, you might want to position the hinges away from them.

# BOM

4 or 6 3mm x 35mm pins (down to 25mm should still work)
4 or 6 M3x8 SHCS
4 or 6 M4 hammer nuts
8 or 12 M3x8 BHCS

# Assembly

The holes are designed so that the pins are a tight fit into the extrusion
mounts, and a looser fit into the hinge that attaches to the panels. That
allows you to take the doors off and the pins stay in place. That means
you will have to tap the pins into place with a tool.

Use the SHCS and hammer nuts to attach the extrusion mounts loosely.

Use the jig to drill the holes in the panel. Check twice between drilling.
You may want to mock up the positions by using sticky tape first, then drill
when you are satisfied the doors will open properly.

Attach the hinge to the panel by sandwiching the panel between it and the
reinforcement part, use the BHCS to attach. Don't screw down too hard,
just tight is enough.

Fit the doors onto the pins. Adjust the extrusion mount position so that the
doors are positioned correctly, aligned with each other and open smoothly.
